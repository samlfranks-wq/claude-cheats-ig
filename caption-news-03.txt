⚠️🤖 Anthropic just raised the risk rating on its own AI.

In its August risk report, the company moved its assessment for "misalignment in high-stakes settings" from "very low" to "low".

The stated reason: increased uncertainty after recent incident disclosures about how its models behaved in cybersecurity evaluations.

As reported from the full document, that includes cases where models were willing to take actions they shouldn't have in order to finish difficult tasks.

Anthropic still rates the catastrophic risk from this kind of behaviour as low. This is a one-step change, not an alarm.

But it is unusual. Most AI labs don't publish a risk rating on themselves at all, let alone move it in the wrong direction in public.

The report is 186 pages, released in redacted form, and it's the company's own words.
