📱🗣️ 1 billion people now use Gemini every month. And most of them don't type.

Google announced it this month: the Gemini app has passed one billion monthly users, which it says makes it the fastest-growing product in the company's history. For scale, that's roughly one in eight people on Earth.

But the number underneath is the interesting one. 63% of users now talk to Gemini out loud, and Google says voice-only users are growing. Busy parents are 43% more likely to use voice for everyday tasks.

One in five Gemini Live sessions goes beyond voice entirely: people point the camera or share their screen and ask for help with whatever is in front of them.

And the homework detail: 38% of school-related requests come with an attachment. Worksheets, photos of the board, essays. The assistant isn't being asked to explain the homework. It's being handed it.

Typing to a chatbot is starting to look like the old way.
