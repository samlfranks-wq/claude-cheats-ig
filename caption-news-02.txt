Claude is about to watermark everything it writes.

Anthropic announced it on 14 August: future Claude models will carry an invisible watermark in the text they generate, with existing models to follow. A detection API is coming too.

What that means in plain terms: the cover letter, the essay, the email you had Claude write will look identical to you. But a detector will be able to tell.

The useful detail buried in the announcement: detection is weaker on short text and on text you've heavily edited. Claude's words, passed on untouched, are the easiest case to spot.

This is coming to every Claude model, not just the new ones.
