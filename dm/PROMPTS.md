Hey! Here are all 7 👇 Add them to the end of any message to Claude.

1. "Ask me questions before you start." Stops it guessing what you meant.
2. "Explain it like I'm new to this." Plain words, no jargon.
3. "Give me three options, then pick the best one and say why."
4. "What would an expert disagree with here?" Gets you the honest critique.
5. "Cut this in half without losing anything important."
6. "Turn this into a checklist I can follow."
7. "What am I missing?" The one that catches the mistakes.

Tip: use 1 at the start of a task and 7 at the end. Save this message so you've always got them.
